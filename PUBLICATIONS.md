```text
Make your P6 schedule easier to review.

Not everyone who needs to understand a project schedule has Primavera P6.

A PDF is useful for a fixed view. But a reviewer may need to find an activity, expand a WBS, or see what comes before and after a task.

P6 XER Viewer Gantt Chart brings the activity table, WBS, and Gantt chart into your browser.

For a procurement review, for example, you can find a package, inspect its dates and total float, and follow its links to installation activities.

You can also:
• Search activities and filter by total float.
• Export the full schedule or a filtered subset as an interactive HTML file.
• Export schedule tables and relationship information to Excel.

The HTML snapshot can be opened by a colleague without importing the original XER. A full export includes activities hidden by your filters, so choose the export scope deliberately.

The viewer processes files locally in the browser. It displays the imported schedule; CPM calculations and rescheduling remain in P6.

The package includes a synthetic sample schedule and a short guide.

Viewer, sample, and guide:
https://github.com/indrapandu/project-toolkit
```
